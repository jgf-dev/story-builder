# Vacation with My Straight Brother - Scene 3: Dinner and Drama

## AUDIO PROFILE
- **Narrator (Levi)**: puck
- **Ewan**: charon
- **Jace**: fenrir
- **Kerry**: aoede

## THE SCENE
- **Setting**: Cabin Kitchen and Dining Table.
- **Mood**: Friendly, casual, domestic, transitioning to tense, hostile, and awkward.
- **Narrative Perspective**: First-person (Levi).

## DIRECTOR'S NOTES
- **Levi** as narrator is warm, reflective, and appreciative of his brother's acceptance. In character, he is hospitable and helpful.
- **Jace** starts off very friendly, complimentary, and casual. As he drinks more, he becomes stubborn and defensive when confronted by Kerry.
- **Kerry** is tense and passive-aggressive. She whispers with concern about Levi drinking, complains about being tired, and then snaps angrily, stomping away.
- **Ewan** is friendly, supportive, and acts as a stabilizing presence, keeping out of the couple's argument.

## TRANSCRIPT
[Narrator (Levi)]
That first afternoon was mostly both couples unpacking suitcases and making sure we each knew where everything was. Ewan and I had brought most of the food, while Jace and Kerry supplied the alcohol.

[Kerry]
(whispering, tense) I'm not sure we should be contributing to a minor.

[Jace]
(stacking beer, casual) Babe, it's my little brother. Besides, he's not exactly a minor. He is twenty, and it's not like we're going to be driving. He's cool.

[Narrator (Levi)]
He had never called me 'cool' before. Never! It made something unlock inside of me. Something that I liked feeling. Like I was one of his friends or something. As if, finally, after all of these years on the outside looking in, I was being accepted by my cool—and now hunky—older brother.
We grilled burgers for dinner, and I made some homemade seasoned potatoes that everyone raved about. Oil, plenty of salt, rosemary, and garlic powder were all added to the oil where I chopped and mixed the potatoes before frying them off in a cast-iron skillet.

[Jace]
(enthusiastic) Damn, culinary school is paying off, little brother!

[Ewan]
(laughs, teasing) Yeah, he's going to make me fat before he graduates.

[Narrator (Levi)]
Ewan said, laughing. He kissed my cheek as he opened a beer and slid it to me. Kerry's eyes clocked the drink and I left it on the counter as I finished up the food.

[Levi]
(hospitably) Everyone enjoy. There's plenty more where this came from.

[Narrator (Levi)]
I said, plopping down in the seat across from Jace and setting the potatoes beside him.
We enjoyed the meal and afterward we spent the next few hours playing card games until Kerry started to yawn.

[Kerry]
(tugging on Jace's arm, tiredly) I'm getting sleepy, babe. Let's go to bed.

[Jace]
(dismissive, checking phone) Bed? It's barely ten o'clock. I'm not ready for bed yet.

[Kerry]
(exasperated) Come on, babe. We had a long day of travel. I'm tired.

[Narrator (Levi)]
She said. Ewan and I tried to look anywhere else than right at them, but the table where we had the deck of cards spread out wasn't that big.

[Jace]
(annoyed, defensive) Okay, you go on to bed. I'll be there afterwhile. You're embarrassing me.

[Kerry]
(voice raising, angry) I'm embarrassing you? Fine. I'm going to bed. I knew this was a bad idea.

[Narrator (Levi)]
She kicked her seat back, letting the legs scrape across the wooden floor. She stomped off and climbed the stairs.

[Kerry]
(screaming back from the stairs) Don't even bother coming to bed. You can sleep on the sofa!

[Jace]
(standing up, pleading but whipped) Babe, don't do this. Come on, don't be like that.

[Narrator (Levi)]
Their voices faded away until Ewan and I were left sitting at the table, both of us stunned at the sudden change of the evening.

[Ewan]
(softly, cleaning up) Did you know they were having trouble?

[Levi]
(confessed, quiet) I had no idea.

[Narrator (Levi)]
I gathered up the plates as he tossed all the empty bottles into the trash. Jace had consumed more alcohol than food, and that might have been part of the problem. Or, maybe he needed the alcohol to deal with Kerry? I really had no idea.
