
# Interface styles

I've researched current trends in AI-assisted writing and high-fidelity audio production to propose three distinct directions for your app.

Here are three visual and functional directions we can explore:

1. The Ink & Ether Studio (Elegant & Editorial) This direction focuses on a sophisticated, high-contrast aesthetic reminiscent of premium typography and classic publishing. It uses a refined serif typeface for writing and a deep charcoal and parchment palette. The interface is "bimodal," featuring a distraction-free "Zen Lock" mode for drafting that seamlessly transitions into a granular audio "Sculpting" mode where you can adjust the emotional prosody of your AI narrator using elegant, thin-line waveform controls.

2. Ghost Shell (Hyper-Minimal & Adaptive) Ghost Shell is built on the concept of "Adaptive Minimalism." The interface is almost entirely invisible while you're writing, with "Ghost Panes" for research and audio settings only materializing when you pause or hover. It uses a modern sans-serif, a monochromatic "e-ink" inspired palette, and a "Spectral Painting" audio editor that lets you visually scrub and refine the AI voice generation directly within the manuscript's margins.

3. Architect Pro (Structured & Industrial) This direction is for power users who want a "Data-Centric" workflow. It features an "Infinite Canvas" for plotting and world-building that sits alongside a dense, sidebar-rich writing environment. The aesthetic is industrial and technical, with a dark mode "OLED" theme, neon accent colors for dialogue tagging (e.g., whispering, shouting), and a dual-track timeline for precise control over AI voice performance and background soundscapes.
